I am a final-year PhD candidate in Telecommunications Engineering at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), working at the iTEAM Research Institute under the supervision of Prof. David Gómez-Barquero and Prof. Narcís Cardona.

My research lies at the intersection of wireless communications, artificial intelligence, signal processing, reinforcement learning, and computational imaging. I develop practical machine learning and signal processing algorithms for next-generation wireless sensing and communication systems, with applications including localization, beamforming, passive sensing, image reconstruction, and autonomous systems.
